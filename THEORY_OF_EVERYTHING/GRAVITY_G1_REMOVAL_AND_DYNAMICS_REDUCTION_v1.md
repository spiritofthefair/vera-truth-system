# Test 1 — Remove A

## A-specific versus A-general
The original `A` conflated two claims:

`A_HKT` = the specific hypersurface-deformation / canonical constraint structure used in the Hojman-Kuchar-Teitelboim reconstruction of Einstein geometrodynamics.

`A_meta` = the more general requirement that locally allowed transformations compose consistently into a coherent physical evolution, with equivalent gauge/slicing/representation choices not generating contradictory physical histories.

## Contradiction attack on A_HKT
HKT is powerful but not ontology-neutral. Its result is tied to assumptions including canonical variables and an embeddable spacetime/hypersurface setting.

A direct counterexample to treating the HKT algebra as the unique universal primitive is Shape Dynamics: it can reproduce the observable content of general relativity while using spatial diffeomorphism plus local conformal symmetry and a different constraint algebra.

Therefore:

`A_HKT AS UNIVERSAL FUNDAMENTAL PRIMITIVE = DISPROVEN`

This does not disprove HKT or its importance inside canonical GR.

## Removal of A_meta
If every composition/closure requirement is removed, there is no general guarantee that:
- equivalent decompositions of an evolution produce equivalent physical predictions;
- gauge-related descriptions remain physically equivalent;
- constraints remain preserved by evolution;
- local updates assemble into one coherent history;
- the theory avoids path-dependent contradictions caused only by representation choice.

Canonical GR makes this visible through first-class constraint closure and hypersurface-deformation consistency. Modified canonical gravity work likewise finds nontrivial covariance/closure conditions when changing the dynamics.

Therefore:

`A_meta INFORMATION = SURVIVES`

but:

`A_meta AS AN INDEPENDENT PHYSICAL OBJECT = NOT ESTABLISHED`

The better classification is:

`A_meta = ADMISSIBILITY / CONSISTENCY CONDITION ON DYNAMICS`

# Test 2 — Remove C

## What can replace explicit causal structure?
In sufficiently well-behaved Lorentzian spacetimes, causal/timelike structure determines substantial geometric information, including topology and conformal structure. Conversely, a full Lorentzian metric already contains its causal light-cone structure.

Therefore an explicit independent object named `C` can be removed if equivalent causal information is encoded elsewhere, for example in the gravitational state/metric or a deeper relational order.

But removing the information itself fails: a theory with no equivalent notion of allowed influence/reachability cannot reconstruct light cones, horizons, causal propagation, or relativistic ordering.

Classification:

`C AS INDEPENDENT PRIMITIVE = NOT REQUIRED`

`CAUSAL/REACHABILITY INFORMATION = REQUIRED`

---

# Test 3 — Remove M_g

Causal structure alone fixes at most conformal/light-cone information under the usual spacetime assumptions. It does not by itself fix the complete physical scale needed for proper intervals, areas, volumes, horizon entropy normalization, or the strength scale of gravitational interactions.

Thus a separate variable called `M_g` is not necessarily fundamental, but equivalent scale/measure information must be recoverable somewhere in the state and laws.

Classification:

`M_g AS INDEPENDENT PRIMITIVE = NOT REQUIRED`

`MEASURE/SCALE INFORMATION = REQUIRED`

A major open sub-question is whether the missing scale is fundamental, dynamically generated, fixed by boundary/state data, or emergent from a deeper quantum/relational measure.

# Test 4 — Remove Phi

If `Phi` means a separate matter source placed beside gravity, vacuum gravitational solutions show that matter is not required for every gravitational process. Gravitational degrees of freedom can propagate in vacuum.

However, a complete theory must contain a physical state on which dynamics acts, and gravity must couple universally to physical energy-momentum/state content in the low-energy regime. Weinberg's soft-graviton result and equivalence-principle tests strongly constrain this universality.

Therefore:

`Phi AS A SEPARATE MATTER-ONLY PRIMITIVE = NOT REQUIRED`

`PHYSICAL STATE / FLOW INFORMATION = REQUIRED`

The gravitational state itself must be included in the complete state description; gravity cannot be only an external response to non-gravitational matter.

---

# Test 5 — Remove R

Removing all response/backreaction leaves only kinematics. It cannot reproduce:
- matter changing gravitational geometry/state;
- gravitational self-interaction;
- dynamical spacetime;
- propagating gravitational degrees of freedom;
- collapse and horizon formation;
- state-dependent change of future causal reachability.

Deser's self-interaction construction and the nonlinear Einstein dynamics show why a consistent massless spin-2 gravitational field cannot remain merely linear/external. Gravitational-wave observations independently require propagating gravitational dynamics in the verified regime.

Therefore:

`R INFORMATION = INDISPENSABLE`

But `R` is naturally a transition law, not necessarily a primitive object distinct from the Theory-of-Everything dynamics `D`.
