# 1. Why this gate exists

Hawking's original information-loss proposal replaces ordinary pure-state S-matrix evolution with a density-matrix superscattering map. If this is taken as a fundamental law, the theory must specify what kind of map it is and what happens when the black-hole system is entangled with degrees of freedom that never enter the hole.

At the same time, ordinary open-system quantum mechanics allows an observer to see nonunitary reduced evolution even when a larger physical system evolves unitarily.

Therefore:

`OBSERVED / EFFECTIVE NONUNITARITY != FUNDAMENTAL GLOBAL NONUNITARITY`.

But the converse error is also forbidden:

`MATHEMATICAL DILATION != VERIFIED PHYSICAL HIDDEN SECTOR`.

# 2. Map-domain gate comes before Stinespring

A candidate black-hole evolution must first specify its domain.

## Autonomous-channel claim
If the candidate claims one linear map

`E_BH : rho_in -> rho_out`

that acts on an arbitrary admissible input system independently of correlations with external reference systems, then the standard quantum-channel consistency requirement is complete positivity plus trace preservation on that stated domain.

Operational test:

`E_BH tensor I_R`

must preserve positivity for arbitrary allowed reference `R` and allowed entangled inputs.

If it fails on legitimate inputs inside the declared domain, the autonomous-channel claim fails.

## Compatibility-domain reduced dynamics
Pechukas showed that reduced dynamics derived from initially correlated system-environment states need not define a completely positive map on the entire system state space. In that case the physically meaningful map may exist only on a restricted compatibility domain.

Therefore:

`NON-CP EXTENSION OUTSIDE PHYSICAL DOMAIN != AUTOMATIC PHYSICAL INCONSISTENCY`.

But a theory using this escape must identify:
- the complete joint state;
- the allowed compatibility domain;
- the initial correlations that restrict it;
- the joint evolution law;
- why the domain is physically complete for the black-hole process under study.

It may not invoke initial correlations ad hoc after a contradiction appears.

# 3. Mathematical dilation gate

For a completely positive map on the declared operator/state domain, Stinespring-type representation theorems provide a larger-space representation in which the map is obtained from a linear/isometric representation followed by restriction to the system.

In finite-dimensional channel language this is equivalent to representing a CPTP map by an isometry/unitary coupling to an ancillary environment and tracing/restricting the environment.

This yields:

`CPTP MAP -> MATHEMATICAL DILATION EXISTS`.

It does NOT yield:

`CPTP MAP -> THE ANCILLA IS A REAL BABY UNIVERSE / REMNANT / SOFT SECTOR / GRAVITATIONAL MICROSTATE`.

The theorem proves representation, not ontology.

# 5. Nonunitarity is not eliminated by one historical no-go argument

Banks, Peskin, and Susskind studied broad density-matrix modifications motivated by black-hole information loss and argued that their class leads to unacceptable tension with locality or energy-momentum conservation.

That result is an important constraint but not a universal proof that all pure-to-mixed fundamental laws are impossible.

Unruh and Wald later constructed classes of Markovian and non-Markovian models showing that pure-to-mixed evolution can be arranged with suitable locality/causality properties and with ordinary-laboratory energy-momentum violations made unobservably small, and argued that exact energy-momentum conservation is not fundamentally incompatible with causality in all such models.

Therefore:

`BPS DIFFICULTIES != UNIVERSAL THEOREM ELIMINATING FUNDAMENTAL NONUNITARITY`.

A nonunitary Theory-of-Everything branch remains logically open, but it carries a heavy acceptance burden.
